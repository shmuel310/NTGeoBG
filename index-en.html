📜 About this Tool & Scholarly Historical Notes ↓

Methodological Principles & Scholarly Positions:

  • Chronology and Authorship Debates: Composition dates and locations for several books remain active discussions in contemporary NT scholarship (e.g., South vs. North Galatian theories, Pauline authorship of the Pastorals, 2 Peter, and the author of Hebrews). This tool adopts the widely accepted scholarly consensus and traditional historical provenance while annotating alternative perspectives in the dossiers.
  • Archaeological Corroboration: Geographic pins are anchored in verified archaeological excavations, including the famous Corinthian Erastus Inscription (Rom 16:23), Gallio’s Bema (Acts 18:12), the Grand Theater of Ephesus (Acts 19:29), and the Pontic royal tombs.
  • Brand Color Language: Styled strictly in Charcoal (#2B2B2B), Warm White (#F5F3EE), Antique Gold (#C8A96A), Slate Blue (#4A5D73), Ivory (#EAE6DC), and Amber (#D6A84C) to evoke ancient vellum manuscripts through clean modern cartography.
